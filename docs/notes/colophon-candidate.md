# Foilbury colophon candidate

This is an agent-authored local proposal, not an owner aesthetic approval or a completed identity rollout. It replaces the earlier rejected Signal Fold mark on the local website shell, favicon and PWA icons while keeping the earlier assets and their historical application record. Nothing here changes the Foilbury Discord application, the Loftwah guild, card art, audiences, or any saved card render.

The mark is an asymmetric open album: a card pocket faces a short index. The intent is a small publisher's colophon that belongs beside collections rather than competing with their artwork. The wordmark uses outlined Fraunces lettering. The emblems and wordmark are produced by `pnpm brand:generate` from `public/fonts/Fraunces.ttf`; the exact axes and generated-file checksums are in `public/brand/kit/colophon/manifest.json`. Fraunces is by The Fraunces Project Authors under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; its licence is retained as `public/brand/kit/colophon/FONT-LICENSE.txt`. `fontkitten` performs the font-outline extraction; `@resvg/resvg-js` and Sharp rasterise and size exports. No image generation or paid provider was used.

Available local destinations include light/dark shell use, 16/32/48 favicons, 180px touch icon, 192/512 app and maskable icons, and a 1024px Discord avatar candidate. The maskable icons use a solid edge-to-edge background and an inset emblem. `mark.svg`, transparent/reverse marks and wordmark SVGs are scalable vector masters; the generator and font retain editable sources. The generated 16px, 32px and 64px circular-crop studies and built-Worker screenshots are under `.foilbury/evidence/identity/` in this worktree. The icon was iterated from rejected plain `fo` and card-initial marks after independent feedback; the open-album symbol remains a proposal rather than proof that the whole identity is finished.

The local site and historical brand-kit copy were updated. The historic selection JSON and all old assets remain unchanged. `pnpm brand:generate` regenerates the current candidate without rewriting the archive; `pnpm brand:generate:archive` retains the former archive generator as a separate explicit operation. Two consecutive candidate generations produced identical manifests, and before/after hashes of all historic kit files matched. The real Discord avatar/application cover, message artwork kit, purpose-built banners, production site and a full palette/shell redesign remain open. A provisional, art-free 1200 × 630 social preview uses a symbolic stack of 5:7 cards and type; it is wired locally to generic previews, but is not a substitute for an art-led treatment after suitable artwork is reviewed. A separate social study using a retained public-safe showcase still has REWORK findings and remains private evidence only. Do not describe this candidate as a coherent deployed identity or release it as-is.

Browser checks use a no-bundle local Worker and synthetic local D1. For in-context screenshots only, the browser supplies previously recorded local public card-render bytes; that transport is intercepted and the artwork itself has separate REWORK findings. These images do not establish live server, native Discord, physical-device, or user acceptance. The 16/32/64 circles are generated size/crop studies, not Discord-client captures. Verify every destination, source/export checksum, rollback and applicable release gate before any deployment.

## Discord application icon: read-only destination check

On 2026-11-05 the signed-in Foilbury application's General Information page in the Discord Developer Portal stated: 1024 × 1024, 1:1 aspect ratio, PNG/GIF/JPG/WEBP and a maximum of 10 MB. The local `discord-avatar-1024.png` is a 1024 × 1024 PNG and approximately 38 KB. It has not been uploaded or applied; these constraints do not establish the visual result in Discord. The official [Application Resource](https://docs.discord.com/developers/resources/application) documents the app icon field as image data, and the [API reference](https://docs.discord.com/developers/reference#image-data) describes image-data encoding. The portal supplied the destination-specific dimensions and size limit.

The provisional social preview is a 1200 × 630 vector-only composition using the card-colophon and symbolic 5:7 card shapes; it contains no character or collection imagery. Its 400 × 210 preview and centred square crop were inspected, and the small footer is not required to understand the image. The main ink/background contrast is 11.43:1 and secondary ink/background is 5.69:1. The output PNG and editable SVG master are generated by the same script and hashed in the asset manifest; Manrope's SIL OFL 1.1 licence is retained alongside the Fraunces licence. The generic Open Graph default and fallback previews use this candidate locally; card-specific previews still use the authorised published card render where available. The previous images remain untouched.

Forced-colours emulation initially made the masked wordmark disappear; the local candidate now uses `CanvasText` in that mode. The failure and corrected screenshot are retained in the evidence directory. This does not replace testing with a human using assistive technology.

The collector home, collection, inspector and share interfaces have not been redesigned or reviewed in this candidate's context. Card-specific preview artwork remains the existing authorised render. The actual Foilbury Discord application avatar and profile, application/profile banners, and Foilbury welcome, command, reveal and achievement/completion artwork are unchanged. No new destination-specific banner or message-art kit has been produced or applied, and no native Discord client capture exists for this symbol. The unrelated Loftwah guild identity is unchanged. Old brand assets remain in place. A complete issue #26 delivery still requires those applicable web and Discord destinations, their actual constraints and rollback, and independent rendered review; the current art-free social image is a provisional treatment, not full visual identity acceptance.
