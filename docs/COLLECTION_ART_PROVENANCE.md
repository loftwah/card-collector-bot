# Collection art provenance

This record covers the sourced banner and mark artwork for the 17 third-party collections in the published Foilbury catalogue. The four Foilbury-owned collections (Bubbles, Fighter, Protocol 11 and Shared Characters) keep their committed original marks under `public/art/collections/`; they are not third-party art.

The rule is: a collection's identity is its own `banner_url` first and `logo_url` second. `cover_url` is a card cover for card contexts and is never used as the collection identity. No banner or mark in this set is a card render, a gradient, a dot grid, an initial/monogram, a motif or a card collage.

All third-party assets are `kind=brand`, `access_classification=member`, `visibility=private`, `official=0`, `review_status=reviewed` and `origin=sourced`. They are served only to signed-in members with an active membership; they are not public showcase content and are not claimed to be official or licensed.

## Retrieval and verification notes

- Fandom image bytes were fetched through a real Chromium session after the public wiki page, because the `static.wikia.nocookie.net` CDN blocks plain command-line requests. The stored PNG/WebP derivatives were decoded from the fetched source and recorded with their own SHA-256.
- Wikimedia Commons logos are the original SVG files where possible, rendered to transparent PNG at a useful size. The Commons file page and public-domain tag are recorded above.
- The Total Drama Island mark is a crop of the published logo plate in `File:TDILogo.jpg`; the crop is recorded in the source transform and is not a generated replacement.
- No paid generation, AI image generation, stock imagery or card collage was used for this set.
