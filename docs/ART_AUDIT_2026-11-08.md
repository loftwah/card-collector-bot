# Art audit and collection banner replacement — 2026/11/08

Status: **PRODUCTION_VERIFIED for the collection banner/mark class**. Issue #39
remains open for the full card-by-card pass, achievement badges, Discord
thumbnails and official wordmark work.

## Why this pass happened

The reported Danganronpa banner was the clearest regression: a magenta/burgundy
gradient, repeated dots, a thin border, a generic serif title and a `DR` initials
seal. A production contact-sheet review found the same generated banner class on
17 collections. The generator was still the approved path in
`art/collection-art.mjs`, so replacing only Danganronpa would have left the same
failure visible everywhere else.

The owner's standard is visual quality, not provenance theatre. This pass
replaces the worst collection-level class with compositions built from the
collection's actual reviewed card renders, its palette and its own name/context.
It does not generate character illustration, does not use paid generation and
does not claim the rest of #39.

## Method and dispositions

- Inventoried **21 published collections**, **158 cards**, **680 published
  variants**, **680 fronts**, **680 backs**, the collection logos/banners and the
  existing representative card contact sheet.
- Downloaded the production banner bytes and inspected them at normal contact
  sheet size. The before sheet is
  `.foilbury/evidence/art-audit-2026-11-08/banner-contact-sheet.png`.
- Applied the explicit failure class from #39: generic gradient, repeated
  dots/grid/stripes, thin generic border, circle/monogram/initials seal used as
  finished collection illustration.
- Dispositions: `KEEP`, `REPLACED`, `NOT REVIEWED`, `BLOCKED`.
- Composed the replacement from **three actual card fronts** per collection, the
  collection palette and a source-specific character mark cropped from the first
  card. No procedural illustration was added.
- The replacement process is retained in
  `scripts/content/compose-collection-banners.mjs`; the applied IDs are in
  `.foilbury/evidence/art-audit-2026-11-08/replacement/applied.json`.

## What was verified

- 17 replacement banners are 1600×900 PNG; 17 marks are 512×512 PNG. Live
  `/media/asset/...` reads returned HTTP 200 `image/png` for the sampled set.
- The before and after contact sheets were visually inspected:
  `.foilbury/evidence/art-audit-2026-11-08/banner-contact-sheet.png` and
  `.foilbury/evidence/art-audit-2026-11-08/replacement/replacement-contact-sheet.png`.
- Danganronpa is live with the new Monokuma mark and the Kyoko/Monokuma/Gonta
  banner. The demo collector's member-only profile share was recreated and
  rendered with the new composition; stages 1, 3, 10, 15 and 16 of the retained
  Discord sequence were refreshed and read back **17/17 PASS**.
- Collection release versions were preserved (Danganronpa/Vocaloid 6, the other
  member collections 5, originals 1). No card identity, inventory, serial,
  ledger, pool or achievement row was changed by this art pass.

## Honest gaps

- **Full per-card audit:** 158 card identities and 680 variants were not each
  inspected at full resolution in this pass. The representative launch contact
  sheet and the release-6 Gonta/Gakupo proof were inspected; every other card is
  `NOT REVIEWED` for the purposes of #39.
- **Backs and finishes:** the new Gonta/Gakupo fronts and backs were verified;
  the remaining 672 back renders and the full finish matrix still need the
  card-by-card pass.
- **Gonta subtitle truncation:** the rendered card truncates “Ultimate
  Entomologist” to “Ultimate Entomolog…”. It is a card-level typography defect,
  recorded here rather than silently accepted.
- **Achievement badges, Discord thumbnails and OG exports:** not audited in this
  pass.
- **Official wordmarks:** no official wordmark was sourced or claimed. The new
  marks are character crops from reviewed card renders and are labelled as
  Foilbury compositions.
- **Historical shares:** a share snapshot captured before this pass can still
  reference the old banner asset bytes. Those are historical snapshots; new
  shares use the replacement assets.
